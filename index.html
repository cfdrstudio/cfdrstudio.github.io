Even Time

An iPhone app that keeps each player's time on the field during a game, shows who has had less or more than an equal share as it goes, and adds it up across the season. For coaches of junior and community teams in any team sport, with nothing uploaded and no account.

Frisket

A Markdown editor that masks the syntax so the writing shows through. Like a letterpress frisket — a mask cut away only where the ink should fall — the markers never leave the file; it stays plain .md that opens anywhere, simply hidden from view the moment the cursor leaves them. Open any .md file on disk, write, and export to PDF, Word, LaTeX, EPUB or HTML — no vault, no database, no project structure, just files.
